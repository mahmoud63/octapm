# OctaPM UAT Participant Pack

Welcome, and thank you for giving up a day to this.

## What this is

OctaPM is a single place for the records a construction project runs on: the programme, the cost,
the commercial position, what has been bought, what is in the store, the drawings and the quality.
The idea behind it is that these are not separate systems that need reconciling. They are one set
of records, and a change in one of them moves the others.

Today is not a demonstration. We are not going to show you how well it works. We are going to give
you work to do and find out whether you can do it.

## What we are actually testing

Not you. The product.

If you cannot find something, that is the most useful thing that can happen today. It means a
screen is in the wrong place, or something is called by a name that is not the name you use on
site. Every time you get stuck, we learn something we cannot learn any other way.

So please:

- Say out loud what you are looking for and where you expect it to be.
- Do not apologise for getting lost.
- Do not let somebody rescue you too early. Struggling for a few minutes is the evidence.
- Tell us when something is called the wrong thing. You know the trade. We are guessing.

## What is in this release

OctaPM Pilot V1 covers the schedule, site reporting, the bill of quantities, cost control, the
commercial position, procurement, inventory, document control, quality, meetings and reporting,
with company administration behind it.

Two things it does **not** have, so that nobody spends the day looking for them:

- **There is no login for a client or owner.** A client representative cannot be given their own
  view of the project in this release.
- **There is no login for a subcontractor.** Their pay applications and their work are recorded by
  your team, not by them.

If either is part of your real job, you will be given an internal role for today's exercises.

## Getting in

1. Open the address you were sent.
2. Sign in with your email address and the password you were given.
3. Change the password when prompted.
4. You will land on Workspace Control. That is your start page.

You are working in a training organisation. Nothing you do today reaches a live project, so there
is no such thing as breaking it. Please do try things.

## The rules for Level 4

1. You will be given a situation and a result to reach. You will not be told which part of OctaPM
   to use.
2. Nobody will help you for ten minutes.
3. You may ask at any point. Your observer will write down that you asked, and what you asked,
   before answering. This is not a mark against you. It is the measurement.
4. There is no time limit. The target times are for us, not for you.
5. If you reach a point where you would stop and telephone somebody in real life, say so. That is
   a result too.
