# Landing hero reel

Twelve films of engineers on site with their plans, behind the landing hero. They are tinted in
the page but no longer blurred, so they are kept at 960x540 rather than at full size. The reel
fetches each film only when it is about to appear, and the leanest one opens, so the page loads
under a megabyte of video however long the reel grows.
