### Added

- Daily report auto-draft from records, photos and voice (OCTA-AI-DAILY-030): `daily-log-assistant.service.js` collects one calendar day **in the project's time zone** — the daily log as logged (weather + alerts, manpower, equipment, deliveries, visitors, delays, notes), progress submissions, inspections, punch items / observations, safety incidents and the day's photos — plus the dictated / typed notes, and asks the model for a summary, work performed with sources, notes, delays, captions for uncaptioned photos and what is still missing; up to `AI_MAX_PHOTOS` photos are sent **as images** (thumbnails, ≤ 4 MB). **Apply** writes only what the person kept, through `daily-log.service` (summary, note and delay entries) and `attachment.service` (captions; existing ones only with `overwriteCaptions`). Without a provider a **factual draft from the records** is returned. Provider layer: message content blocks (`text` / `image`) mapped to Anthropic, OpenAI and Gemini; `describe()` reports `vision` (`AI_VISION=off`) and `transcription`; `transcribeAudio` (OpenAI `whisper-1`, `AI_TRANSCRIPTION_MODEL`, `AI_TRANSCRIPTION=off`). Runs stored as `AssistantConversation` kind `daily_log_draft` on the day. Endpoints `GET /projects/{id}/daily-logs/ai/status`, `POST …/daily-logs/ai/transcribe` (multipart `audio`), `POST …/daily-logs/{date}/ai/draft`, `POST …/daily-logs/{date}/ai/apply` (540 OpenAPI paths). See `docs/qa/daily-log-assistant.md`.
